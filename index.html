Rules & Strategy

How to Play (One-Word Challenge) ⚡

1

Secret paper with a Place Name (7 people get the same place, 1 person is the Impostor!).

2

Do NOT show your paper to anyone.

3

Say ONLY 1 WORD about your place on your turn! No full sentences, no using the place name itself!

4

Strategic Tip: Don't be too obvious (or the Impostor will guess!), but don't be too vague (or colleagues will suspect you!).

5

At the end, vote: Who is the Impostor?

The Rules Script (Read out loud) 📣

"Hi everyone! We are playing The Impostor: One-Word Challenge Edition!

1. I will give everyone a secret piece of paper with a place written on it.
2. Seven of us have the same place, but one person is the Impostor with a different paper!
3. When it's your turn, you can say ONLY ONE SINGLE WORD about your place.
4. Rules for the word: No full sentences, and don't say the place name itself!
5. Try to choose a clever word: not too obvious, but not completely crazy! After everyone speaks, we vote!"

Host Tips for Strategic Clues ⚡

  • • Warn players: If your word is too easy (e.g., "Popcorn" for Cinema), the Impostor will immediately guess the location!
  • • Warn players: If your word is too random (e.g., "Good"), people will think you are the Impostor!
  • • Impostor Victory Bonus: If the Impostor is voted out, allow them 1 final guess to name the location. If correct, they win!
